Privacy Policy

Last Updated: June 2026

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how Gavendra Singh ("we," "us," or "our") collects, uses, and protects your personal data when you use this website and our consulting services.

2. Data We Collect

When you book a consultation, use the contact form, or interact with our website, we may collect the following personal information:

  • Name
  • Email Address
  • Phone Number (if provided)
  • Consultation Details (information regarding your startup or requirements)
  • Payment Information (processed securely by Razorpay)

3. How We Collect Data

We collect data directly from you when you voluntarily submit it through our website, such as when filling out a contact form or completing a payment via Razorpay Checkout.

4. Purpose of Data Usage

The information we collect is used for the following purposes:

  • To schedule and provide the requested consultation services.
  • To communicate with you regarding your booking or inquiries.
  • To process payments securely.
  • To improve our services and website experience.

5. Payment Processing (Razorpay)

All payments are securely processed by Razorpay. We do not store your credit card details, UPI IDs, or other sensitive financial information on our servers. Razorpay's privacy policy applies to the payment data you provide during checkout.

6. Data Protection Measures

We implement appropriate security measures to protect your personal information from unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction. However, please note that no method of transmission over the internet or electronic storage is 100% secure.

7. Third-Party Services

We may use third-party services, such as EmailJS for sending confirmation emails and Google Meet for conducting sessions. These third parties have their own privacy policies governing how they handle your data.

8. User Rights

You have the right to request access to the personal data we hold about you and ask for it to be corrected or deleted. To exercise these rights, please contact us using the details below.
