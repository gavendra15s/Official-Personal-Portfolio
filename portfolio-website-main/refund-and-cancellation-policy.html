Refund & Cancellation Policy

Last Updated: June 2026

1. Cancellation & Rescheduling

We value both your time and ours. If you need to cancel or reschedule your session, the following rules apply:

  • More than 24 hours notice: You may cancel or reschedule your session without any penalty. A full refund or a free reschedule option will be provided.
  • Within 24 hours notice: Cancellations made less than 24 hours before the scheduled session time are not eligible for a refund. We may offer a rescheduling option at our sole discretion, but it is not guaranteed.

2. Missed Sessions (No-Shows)

If you fail to attend a scheduled session (no-show) without prior notice, no refund will be issued, and the session will be considered fulfilled.

3. Consultant Cancellations & Technical Issues

If we must cancel the session due to unforeseen circumstances or technical issues on our end, you will be offered the choice of:

  • A free reschedule at a mutually convenient time.
  • A full refund of the session fee.

4. Refund Processing Timeline

Approved refunds will be processed back to your original payment method via Razorpay. Please allow 7–10 business days for the refunded amount to reflect in your bank account or credit card statement.

5. How to Request a Refund or Reschedule

To request a cancellation, reschedule, or refund, please contact us directly using the details provided below. Be sure to include your name, booking date, and transaction/payment ID.
